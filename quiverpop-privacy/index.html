Quiver Pop – Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Quiver Pop: Arrow Puzzle ("the game") handles and why. The game is published by Simple Future AB ("we"). Contact us at info@simplefuture.se.

Short version

  • We have no user accounts and no servers of our own. We don't receive or store your personal data.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads. If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you're asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: level, coins, boosters, settings (sound, vibration), daily reward streak, and a record of purchases you've made, so that each purchase is only granted once. This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads: optional video ads you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads between levels (unless you buy Remove Ads). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps).

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. Depending on your choice, you get personalized ads or non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Menu → About → Privacy settings.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase went through (which product it was, an order number and a purchase token) so that it can grant and restore what you bought. We never receive your name, email address or payment details.

Legal basis

For players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, personalized advertising is based on your consent. Non-personalized advertising, measurement and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in funding a free game. You can withdraw your consent at any time as described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Since we don't hold personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google through their privacy tools. You're always welcome to contact us at info@simplefuture.se. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, for example by adding analytics, we'll update this policy and the date at the top.
